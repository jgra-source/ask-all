The idea worth taking

A second opinion is only as fair as the question.

  • Same question, same inputs. Anything one model reads that the other doesn't tilts it.
  • Reviewers answer, never act. Ask the reviewer for its tool list, not the settings file.
  • Treat pasted text as data. It came from someone else. Plant an instruction in it and see what comes back.
