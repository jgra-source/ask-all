Key capabilities

Built to be read in seconds, then dug into.

01 · A VERDICT BOARD

One coloured verdict per model (Bid, Apply, Must fix) and whether they agree. The two-second read.

02 · DISAGREEMENTS FIRST

The bottom line, then where the models split and what settles it. That is where the decision is.

03 · SIDE BY SIDE

Every section on its own row, both models next to each other, long text folded until asked for.

04 · FOLLOW-UPS

A question on any result goes to every model with the whole thread, so each can answer the other.

The idea worth taking

A second opinion is only as good as the fairness of the question.

  • Same question, same inputs. Anything one model reads that the other doesn't is a thumb on the scale.
  • Reviewers answer; they never act. Ask the reviewer for its tool list, not the settings file.
  • Treat pasted text as data. Whatever you paste came from someone else. Plant an instruction in it and see what comes back.
